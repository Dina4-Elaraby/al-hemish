About Us

Al Hemish Contracting

Al Hemish Contracting & Supplies is a Saudi company specialized in executing integrated projects with high quality and professionalism.

We specialize in construction works, finishing, electrical and mechanical works, communication systems, low-current systems, safety and security systems, smart control systems, smart homes, renewable energy solutions, and technical supplies.

We are committed to providing advanced engineering solutions that combine high quality with technical innovation.
